One setting to get started

  1. Open this extension’s settings.
  2. Turn on Allow in incognito.
  3. Reopen No Incognito to check the status.

This permission lets No Incognito detect and close private windows, including any that are already open. Save anything you need first.

What this can and can’t block

Incognito windows close automatically after they open. A window may flash briefly, and a page may start loading before it closes. Chrome’s incognito menu and keyboard shortcut remain available.

Disabling this extension or its incognito permission stops the blocking. Other profiles, Guest mode, and other browsers are unaffected.

To disable incognito itself, an administrator must set Chrome’s IncognitoModeAvailability policy to 1. Read Google’s policy guide ↗
